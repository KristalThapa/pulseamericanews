Sex Positions Guide – Detailed Illustrated Positions with Tips

A clear, practical, and detailed reference of popular sex positions with body placement, movement tips, variations, benefits, and comfort advice.

Illustrations are educational diagrams from Wikimedia Commons. Partner A and Partner B are used so the guide works for any couple.

Before You Begin

  • Consent & communication — Agree on what you both want and keep checking in. A simple shared word for “slow down” or “stop” makes everything safer and more relaxed.
  • Warm-up — Spend time on kissing, touching, and oral. Arousal makes every position more comfortable and pleasurable.
  • Lubrication — Use a quality water-based lubricant and re-apply whenever friction increases.
  • Props — Firm pillows, folded towels, or a wedge cushion improve angle and comfort in many positions.
  • Listen to your body — Stop immediately if anything feels painful or wrong.

Classic Positions

Missionary

Beginner · Slow to medium · Best for closeness & eye contact

Classic missionary illustration

Partner A lies on their back with legs open or raised. Partner B kneels or lies between their legs, facing them, and enters from the front.

How it works: Partner B supports most of their weight on forearms or hands so Partner A can breathe and move freely. Partner A can wrap legs around Partner B’s waist, plant feet on the bed, or rest them on Partner B’s shoulders.

Benefits: Easy kissing and eye contact, simple for beginners, good emotional connection.

Pro tip: Place a firm pillow under Partner A’s hips. This simple change often improves the angle and increases pleasure for both partners.

Doggy Style

Beginner · Medium to fast · Best for depth & hand access

Doggy style illustration

Partner A is on all fours or resting on elbows and knees. Partner B kneels or stands behind and enters from the rear.

How it works: Partner A keeps knees roughly hip-width. Partner B holds the hips or lower back to control rhythm and depth. Either partner can reach around for additional stimulation.

Variations: Chest down with hips high, one knee lifted, Partner A pushing back to set the pace.

Tip: Lowering the chest usually creates a more intense angle. Always have a clear stop signal.

Cowgirl

Beginner · Partner A controls the pace · Best for control & clitoral contact

Cowgirl illustration

Partner B lies on their back. Partner A straddles them facing forward and moves by lifting, rocking, or grinding.

How it works: Partner A can kneel or place feet flat. Rocking and circular grinding usually require less energy than full up-and-down movement.

Tip: Leaning forward while grinding often gives the best combination of depth and external stimulation.

Reverse Cowgirl

Intermediate · Slow to medium

Same base as cowgirl, but Partner A faces away from Partner B’s head.

The angle of entry is different, so shorter, more controlled movements often feel better.

Go slowly at first. Some people love this angle; others find it uncomfortable.

Spooning

Beginner · Slow · Best for intimacy & low energy

Educational composite

Both partners lie on their sides facing the same direction. Partner B enters from behind while bodies stay close.

Movement is small and gentle. Excellent for morning sex, when tired, or during pregnancy.

Raising the upper leg or placing a pillow under it opens the angle and prevents numbness.

Face-to-Face & Intimate

Side-by-Side Face-to-Face

Both partners lie on their sides facing each other. Legs interlock or one leg drapes over the partner’s hip.

Neither person carries the other’s weight, making it sustainable for long sessions.

Excellent for deep kissing and emotional connection.

Lotus / Seated Face-to-Face

Partner B sits. Partner A sits in their lap facing them, legs wrapped around the waist.

Focus is on closeness, slow grinding, and eye contact rather than fast thrusting.

Best on a firm surface. Support each other’s backs with your hands.

Yab-Yum

A more upright, meditative seated position. Chests stay close. Movement is very slow and intentional.

Ideal for mindful or tantric-style sex.

From Behind & Rear Entry

Kneeling Rear-Entry

Both partners kneel. Partner A leans forward onto hands or elbows. Partner B enters from behind.

More upright than classic doggy — easier to hold each other and talk.

Prone Bone

Partner A lies flat on their stomach. Partner B lies on top and enters from behind.

Partner B should keep weight on elbows so Partner A can breathe easily.

Standing Doggy

Partner A bends forward (hands on wall, bed, or table). Partner B stands behind.

Great for spontaneous moments. Bend knees to adjust for height differences.

Standing & Edge Positions

Standing Face-to-Face

Standing illustration

Both partners stand. Partner A usually leans against a wall. One partner may lift a leg or bend knees for better alignment.

Works best when partners are similar in height. Short, controlled movements are safer.

Butterfly / Edge of the Bed

Partner A lies on their back at the edge of the bed. Partner B stands or kneels between their legs.

Very versatile — easy to combine with oral or manual stimulation.

Table or Counter

Partner A sits or lies on a strong table or counter. Partner B stands between their legs.

Make sure the surface is completely stable and clear of objects.

Legs-Up & Deeper Positions

Anvil & Legs on Shoulders

Partner A lies on their back and raises both legs toward the chest or places them on Partner B’s shoulders.

Start shallow and go slowly. The angle can feel intense very quickly.

Mating Press

Partner A’s legs are pushed back toward the head. Partner B lies on top and presses in deeply.

Requires good flexibility. Warm up first and stop at the first sign of strain.

Piledriver (Advanced)

Advanced position where Partner A’s weight rests on shoulders and neck. High risk of injury.

Not recommended for most people. Safer deep-angle alternatives exist.

Lazy & Low-Effort Positions

Spoons with Leg Lift

Classic spooning with the upper leg raised higher. Opens the angle while keeping the body relaxed.

Perfect for tired nights. A pillow under the raised knee helps prevent cramps.

Modified Missionary (Pillow Boost)

Standard missionary with one or two firm pillows under the hips. One of the simplest and most effective upgrades.

Firm pillows work better than soft ones that compress too much.

Scissors

Partners lie on their sides facing opposite directions with legs interleaved. Movement is mostly grinding.

Good for clitoral stimulation and a different kind of pressure.
